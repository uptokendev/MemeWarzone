title: Glossary
description: Common MemeWarzone terms explained in plain language.
---

## Arena

The post-graduation competitive layer for Normal Battles, Tournaments, Major War League, Quarterly Championship, Boosts, sponsorships, and related claims.

## Arena eligibility

The separate authority that decides whether a token may enter financial competition. Project ownership verification does not grant Arena eligibility.

## Bonding Curve

The native pre-graduation market mechanism used by a MemeWarzone-launched campaign before it moves into its permanent post-graduation market.

## Boost

A paid community-support action used by specific Arena competition formats. Normal Battle Boost and Vote Tournament Boost have different scoring rules from UpVotes.

## Campaign

A native MemeWarzone launch object that carries on-chain generation, launch state, market context, and related product signals.

## Command Center

The private wallet/account console for project management, rewards, claims, Recruiter, Squad, and other account tools according to the active product release.

## DRAFT

The preparation state before a native campaign is deployed or before live trading opens, depending on the active flow.

## Epoch

A fixed competition or reward period used for standings, publication, settlement, or claims.

## Final Salvo

The free-vote tie-break format used only when a Vote Tournament ends regulation in an exact tie.

## Graduation Market

The selected permanent quote market for a native campaign after bonding. It is revalidated at graduation and is separate from Import or Arena eligibility.

## IMPORTED

A project label showing that the token already existed before it was registered with MemeWarzone. It does not mean the token was launched by MemeWarzone.

## Major War League (MWL)

The monthly chain-specific post-graduation league layer in Arena.

## OWNER VERIFIED

A project ownership state showing that the current MemeWarzone project-page ownership process has verified who can control the page. It is not a token safety guarantee or Arena approval.

## POST

The market stage after a native MemeWarzone campaign has graduated into its permanent post-graduation venue.

## PRE

The live native bonding / pre-graduation campaign state.

## Quarterly Championship

The continuous chain-specific standing across a quarter. It is not the canonical quarterfinal / semifinal / final knockout bracket.

## Recruiter

A growth operator who brings users into MemeWarzone through tracked attribution and can participate in the configured Recruiter reward system.

## Token Import

The onboarding path for an existing token. Import can create or claim a MemeWarzone project page without relaunching the token or fabricating native campaign economics.

## UpVote

A paid discovery/visibility action used where the current runtime enables it. It is not the same as an Arena Boost and is not a safety label.

## Vote Tournament

A tournament format where 24-hour regulation is scored by Vote Points rather than market-cap, holder, or volume metrics.

## WARZONE ACCESS LOCKED

The state shown when a registered/imported project does not currently have active financial Arena access. The project can still have a public identity without Battles, Tournaments, Boosts, or Arena claims being enabled.

## War Trade Room

The market discovery and trading surface that normalizes campaign/project context across supported market stages and chains.
