A battle is a timed fight between two coins on the same chain. One wins, one loses, and the result counts in the leagues.

![The Battles page](/images/docs/battles.png)

![A battle page](/images/docs/battle-page.png)

## The Battles list

Open **Warzone**, then **Battles**. The tabs are **Live**, **Upcoming**, **My battles** and **Finished**. You can filter by chain (including all chains) and by battle type.

The first live battle is shown large at the top. Each battle card shows both coins, the countdown, the prize pool and the Vote and Boost buttons. Press **Open fight** to open the battle page.

## Battle types

| Type | How it is decided | Length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vote Battle | free votes and boosts | 6, 12, 24 or 48 hours |
| Metrics battle | change in market cap, holders and trading volume during the fight, plus boosts | 24 hours, 3 days or 7 days |

In a Vote Battle each wallet has one free vote, worth 1 point. A boost costs $1 and adds 2 points. There is no limit on boosts.

A metrics battle scores the change during the fight, in percent, so a bigger coin does not win just for being bigger. Both coins need live market data.

## Start a battle

The owner of a coin challenges another coin:

1. Press **Challenge a coin** on the Battles page, or **Challenge** on a coin page.
2. Pick your coin and an opponent on the same chain. The opponent needs a verified owner who can answer.
3. Choose the battle type, the buy-in and the fight length.
4. Review and send the challenge.

The other owner sees the challenge as a popup and in their Command Center. They can accept, decline or send a counter offer with a different buy-in or length. The fight goes live only after both agree, and each side pays the buy-in once both have agreed.

## The battle page

The battle page has the fight at the top: both coins, the countdown, the score, and the Vote and Boost buttons. Updates arrive live.

Below are the tabs:

- **Live**: a comment box and one timeline of comments and activity, newest first
- **Comments**: all comments
- **Supporters**: wallets ranked by how much they boosted
- **Rules**: how this battle is scored
- **Pool** (on a phone): the prize pool

On a computer the prize pool, the top supporters and the rules sit in a column next to the tabs.

## Comments

Connect your wallet and write in the comment box. A comment is up to 280 characters. If you used your free vote in the battle, your comment shows which side you voted for.

One wallet signature covers commenting, and your other social actions, for 30 days. Read **[One signature for social actions](/social/signing)**.

## Prize pool

The prize pool is made of both buy-ins plus the boosts. When the battle is settled, the winning coin's owner claims 75% of both buy-ins and 90% of the boosts from the Command Center, under **Rewards and claims**. 20% of the buy-ins goes to the Major War League prize pool and 5% to the protocol.

Nothing is sent automatically. Read **[War Pools](/arena/war-pools)**.

## League points

- Ranked battles: the winner gets 3 points, the loser 1.
- Vote Battles are always ranked.
- An unranked metrics battle is called Open War. Its points count half: 1.5 for a win, 0.5 for a loss.

Read **[Major War League](/arena/major-war-league)**.

## Share a battle

Press **Share** on the battle card. You can share on X, download a share image of the battle, or copy the battle link. The post text quotes the current vote tally.
