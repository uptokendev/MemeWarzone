The Major War League is the monthly table for graduated coins. Coins earn points in ranked battles. The top 8 play the quarterly finals.

![The Major War League page](/images/docs/major-war-league.png)

Open **Warzone**, then **Major War League**. The page follows the selected chain.

## What you see

- the season, the week and the phase
- **Month ends in**: a countdown to 00:00 UTC on the 1st of next month
- the prize pool
- **Top command**: the coin in first place
- **Standings**: every ranked coin, with points and status
- **Your tokens**: coins your connected wallet created
- **Previous months**: earlier seasons

The tabs are **Regular season** and **Quarter Finals**.

## Points

| Result | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Ranked win | 3 |
| Ranked loss | 1 |
| Open War win | 1.5 |
| Open War loss | 0.5 |

Open War is an unranked metrics battle; its points count half. Points reset each season. Read **[Live Battles](/arena/live-battles)**.

## Status in the table

A line in the standings marks the quarterly finals cut. The status column shows whether a coin is in, qualified or out against that cut.

## Quarter Finals

The Quarter Finals tab shows the projected field of 8 and the projected matchups: 1 against 8, 4 against 5, 3 against 6 and 2 against 7. It also shows the last coin inside the cut and the first coin outside it.

A coin needs at least 3 finished fights in the quarter to appear in the projected field.

## Prize pool

20% of every battle buy-in goes to the league.

- On BNB Chain and Robinhood Chain each deposit is split: 60% to the current month and 40% to the quarterly finals. The page shows **Prize pool this month** and the **Quarterly finals pot**.
- On Solana one pot pays both the monthly round and the quarterly finals. The page shows **Prize pool · monthly + finals**.

Claims for league prizes live in the Command Center. Read **[Epochs & Claims](/rewards/epochs-and-claims)**.
