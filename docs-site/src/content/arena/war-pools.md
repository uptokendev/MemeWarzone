---
title: War Pools
description: How a battle's prize pool is filled, split and claimed.
---

Every battle has a prize pool, called the war pool. It holds the buy-ins of both coins and the boosts people buy during the fight.

## How the pool fills

1. A coin owner sends a challenge with a buy-in.
2. When both sides agree, each side pays the buy-in into the pool.
3. During the fight, anyone can buy boosts. Each boost adds to the pool.

The battle card and the battle page show the pool as it grows.

## How the pool is split

| Part | Goes to |
| --- | --- |
| 75% of both buy-ins | the winning coin's owner |
| 90% of the boosts | the winning coin's owner |
| 20% of the buy-ins | the Major War League prize pool |
| 5% of the buy-ins | the protocol |

## Claiming

Nothing is sent automatically. When the battle is settled, the owner of the winning coin opens Command Center, **Rewards and claims**, and claims the prize with the wallet that owns the coin.

Before the result is final the row says it is waiting for the on-chain result. After you claim, it shows **Claimed**.
