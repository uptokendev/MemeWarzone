This page moved. Read **[Epochs & Prizes](/leagues/epochs-and-prizes)**.

Weekly epochs run from Monday 00:00 UTC to the next Monday. Monthly epochs run from the 1st of the month 00:00 UTC to the next 1st. Prizes are claimed from your Command Center. Read **[Epochs & Claims](/rewards/epochs-and-claims)**.
