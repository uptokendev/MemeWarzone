title: Squad Pool

---

Squad Pool is the weekly squad reward lane.

It rewards eligible contribution from users who are part of a recruiter connected squad. It is not a flat split and it is not a reward for showing up once.


## Who this is for

Squad Pool is for users who are linked into a valid squad path and meet the rules for the current epoch.

## How it works at a high level

- activity is tracked during the epoch
- eligible squad contribution is scored
- squad level results are produced
- member level reward states become visible in the wallet flow
- claimable rewards move into the claim board after publication

## What users need to understand

Squad Pool rewards contribution.
It does not guarantee that every squad member receives the same result.

If a wallet is detached from a squad path or becomes ineligible under the current rules, that will affect the reward lane for that epoch.

## Where to check status

Use Command Center for your private squad and claim view.
Use public leaderboards for the public competition view.
