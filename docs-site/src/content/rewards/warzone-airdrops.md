Warzone Airdrops are a weekly reward for active traders and creators who are not the biggest wallets.

Each chain runs its own airdrop with its own pot. On Solana the Command Center tab shows it as SOL Airdrops, on BNB Chain as BNB Airdrops.

## Who can win

Two groups can qualify during a weekly window:

- traders with real buying and selling activity
- creators whose coins attract real buyers

A few rules keep it fair:

- Traders need a minimum volume, a minimum number of trades and activity on more than one day in the week.
- A creator's own buys do not count toward their coin.
- Wallets flagged by our security checks are left out.
- A wallet that won sits out for 14 days before it can win again.
- Very large activity is capped, so the pot spreads over more people.

The limits are the same in US dollars on every chain. The exact numbers are not published, so they cannot be gamed.

## When it runs

- The weekly window closes on Monday at 00:00 UTC.
- Winners are picked from the closed week and published shortly after.
- The pot is split over the winners, so a bigger pot means more winners.

## Claiming

- Winning drops show up in Command Center under Rewards and claims.
- Connect the wallet that won and claim there.
- Unclaimed drops go back into the pot after 60 days, so they help a later week.

## Where to look

- Command Center > Airdrops: the running week, the current pot, last winners and a preview of who is eligible.
- The Home sidebar: this week's airdrop pot for the selected chain.
