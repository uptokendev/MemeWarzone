MemeWarzone gives creators the same core launch choices across **BNB Chain, Solana, and Robinhood Chain**.

The chain changes wallet and execution mechanics. It does not change the meaning of Direct, Draft, Prepare Mode, scheduled launch, PRE, graduation, or POST.

![Direct and Draft launch routes](/images/docs/creator-direct-draft-routes.png)

## Direct

Direct is for campaigns that are ready to move.

Use Direct when:

- the name and ticker are final
- the logo and campaign media are ready
- official links have been checked
- the Graduation Market choice is understood where available
- the community knows the launch plan
- you are ready to complete the chain-specific wallet transaction

The normal Direct flow is:

1. choose BNB, Solana, or Robinhood
2. complete the campaign form
3. review launch and Graduation Market settings
4. confirm the wallet transaction
5. open Token Details after the campaign is accepted
6. trade through native bonding in BNB, SOL, or ETH according to chain

## Draft / Prepare Mode

Draft gives you a staging area before trading opens.

Use Draft when you want to prepare the campaign, build a public promotion page, coordinate the community, collect followers, or control the launch window before opening the market.

The normal Draft flow is:

1. choose the chain
2. create the draft/project identity
3. complete the campaign identity and official links
4. prepare the public page
5. share Prepare Mode while building attention
6. deploy when ready
7. open trading according to the chosen immediate or scheduled launch behavior

## Scheduled launch

Scheduled launch follows the same product rule on all three chains.

The campaign is deployed before the future launch time. The token/campaign exists before trading opens, and the authoritative `launchAt` time controls when trading becomes available. It is not a second backend deployment transaction at launch time.
