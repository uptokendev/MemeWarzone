---
title: Direct and Draft Launches
description: Choose the right launch route and move a coin from setup to live trading.
---

MemeWarzone gives creators two launch routes: Direct and Draft. You pick one in the first Create step, **Path**.

## Direct

Direct is for coins that are ready to launch now.

Use Direct when:

- the name and ticker are final
- the image is ready
- official links have been checked
- the community knows the launch plan
- you are ready to complete the wallet transaction

The normal Direct flow is:

1. select the chain
2. go through the Create steps
3. check everything on the Review step
4. confirm the wallet transaction
5. open the coin page once the coin is live

## Draft

Draft gives you a staging area before trading opens.

Use Draft when you want a public page before trading opens, to gather a community and choose the launch moment. Drafts show on the **Drafts** tab on Coins and in search as PRE-LAUNCH.

The normal Draft flow is:

1. select the chain
2. go through the Create steps and save the draft
3. finish the promotion setup: message, links, timing and founder note
4. share the public draft page to build attention
5. push the coin live when the launch window opens

## Before you push live

Check the chain, ticker, image, official links and launch time before the final wallet action.

Once the coin trades, people act on what the page says. Clean information at launch prevents confusion later.

## After launch

Open the coin page to follow trades, holders, UpVotes and curve progress. Post your first update as the coin in the **Posts** tab.
