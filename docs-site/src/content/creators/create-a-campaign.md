A native MemeWarzone campaign can be created on **BNB Chain, Solana, or Robinhood Chain**.

The creator journey is intentionally consistent across the three chains. The selected network changes wallet and execution mechanics, bonding asset, and post-graduation venue, but not the core product flow.

## Choose the chain first

| Chain | Native bonding asset | Post-grad venue architecture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BNB Chain | BNB | Topaz |
| Solana | SOL | Meteora |
| Robinhood Chain | ETH | chain-local V3 infrastructure |

Choose the correct chain before finalizing campaign settings. Ticker identity, wallet authorization, launch state, Graduation Market, and later Warzone identity all remain chain-aware.

## What to prepare

Before you open the campaign flow, have these items ready:

- name
- ticker
- logo
- banner or supporting image
- short public description
- official website if you have one
- official X account
- official Telegram or Discord
- launch note or announcement copy
- preferred launch timing
- Graduation Market preference where the active flow offers one

## Direct, Draft, and scheduled launch

**Direct** is for a campaign that is ready to deploy and open through the normal launch flow.

**Draft / Prepare Mode** lets the creator build the project page, socials, artwork, followers, and promotion before deployment.

**Scheduled launch** deploys before the selected future start time and uses authoritative launch timing to open trading. It does not require a second developer-wallet deployment when the clock reaches zero.

Read **[Direct and Draft Launches](/creators/direct-and-draft)**.

## Graduation Market

Bonding remains native in BNB, SOL, or ETH.

The Graduation Market controls the permanent quote market after the campaign reaches the graduation threshold. MemeWarzone only offers markets that meet the current chain-local policy, and the selected market is revalidated at graduation.

## Creator safety

Creator tier, cooldown, wallet restrictions, live-campaign limits, cluster/risk controls, and chain identity can all affect whether a launch action is allowed.

Those controls are security inputs, not merely frontend labels.

## Final check before launch

Before the final wallet action, confirm:

- BNB, Solana, or Robinhood is selected correctly
- the connected wallet is correct
- ticker and campaign identity are final
- artwork and public links are correct
- launch timing is correct
- the selected Graduation Market is understood
- the wallet request matches the action you intended

## Already launched elsewhere?

Do not create a duplicate token merely to join MemeWarzone.

Use **[Import an Existing Token](/import)** to register an existing BNB, Solana, or Robinhood token and build its project identity.
