title: Creator Earnings
description: How creators earn from their coin and where to claim it.
---

Creator earnings come from two places.

First, a share of the trading fees while your coin trades.
Second, the 2% creator reserve (20M tokens). On today's launches it is released when the coin graduates. On gen-8 BNB Chain and Robinhood Chain launches it can be released once the coin has been graduated for at least 1 hour and the price is at or above the graduation market cap. The 2% graduation fee goes to MemeWarzone's fee routing, not to the creator.

On chains and launch types that offer it, you choose in the **Bond** step of Create what happens to your share of the trade fee: keep it, give it to holders, split it, or use it for buyback and burn.

You claim creator earnings from your Command Center, under **Rewards and claims**. On gen-8 you claim your fee share to any wallet you choose. You can hand your creator rights to another wallet in two steps: propose, then accept. MemeWarzone cannot change this.

## The numbers

| Source | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Trades on your bonding curve | 5.6% of the 2% trade fee, which is 0.112% of every buy and sell |
| Graduation | the 2% creator reserve, 20M tokens |
| Pool fees after graduation | 80% of the fees your locked liquidity earns |

On gen-8 BNB Chain and Robinhood Chain launches against BNB or ETH:

| Source | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Trades before graduation | your chosen share of the 2% trade fee, paid in BNB or ETH, never in the coin |
| Trades after graduation | 0.5% of every buy and sell (half of the 1% trade fee). MemeWarzone gets the rest |
| Graduation | the 2% creator reserve, 20M tokens, after the 1 hour rule above |

Your fee choice (keep, give to holders, split, buyback and burn) still applies. Solana stays on the table above. Coins paired with a stock or another quote token keep the current rules for now. Coins launched before the gen-8 release keep the rules they launched with.

Coins launched before October 2026 earn 5% of the trade fee (0.10% of every trade). Read **[Fee Routing](/fees/fee-routing)** and **[After Graduation](/fees/after-graduation)**.

## How creators should think about earnings

The real creator goal is not one isolated payout.
It is a clean launch, real attention, steady activity, and a coin that strengthens your profile for the next launch.

## What grows creator value

- clearer launch execution
- stronger community response
- better discovery performance
- stronger reputation over repeated campaigns
- posting updates and taking part in battles and leagues
