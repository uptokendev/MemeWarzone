Creators start the fight. A strong creator does more than deploy a token: they prepare the page, launch cleanly, post updates, and keep attention long enough for the coin to matter.

## Two ways in

- **Launch a coin** on MemeWarzone with Direct or Draft. Read **[Create a Campaign](/creators/create-a-campaign)**.
- **Import a coin** you already run on BNB Chain, Solana or Robinhood Chain, then claim it as the owner. Read **[Import and claim a coin](/creators/imported-coins)**.

## The creator path

1. choose Direct or Draft, or import
2. get the page and links right
3. launch with the right wallet on the right chain
4. post updates as the coin and answer people in the War Room
5. graduate, then take on other coins in battles and the leagues

## What good creators keep clear

- one clear message
- one set of official links
- one launch plan
- one reason for people to care

## Your coin page

As the owner you can:

- post updates as the coin in the **Posts** tab, and share them to the home feed
- pin one post to the top
- edit links, tags and the Story from **Edit page** in Command Center, **My coins**
- show or hide auto updates for launch, graduation and battle results
- challenge other coins once your coin has graduated

Read **[Coin pages and creator updates](/social/creator-updates)**.

## What creators gain

- a market around their coin
- visibility on Home, Coins, search and the Warzone pages
- a profile that carries their track record to the next launch
- creator earnings under the current fee model
