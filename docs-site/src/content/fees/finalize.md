---
title: Finalize fee
description: Fee charged on graduation and finalization.
---

The finalize fee is the fee applied when a coin graduates: it leaves the bonding curve and moves to its DEX pool.

It is part of the graduation process, not a separate surprise action after the fact.

## When it applies

The finalize fee applies only when the campaign reaches the active graduation target and the finalize path runs.

If the target is not reached, this fee does not apply.

## What it does

The finalize fee gives the router a defined envelope for the transition event.

That envelope can support reward lanes, treasury lanes, and other approved routing destinations under the active policy.

## What it does not mean

Finalize does not guarantee:

- long term market strength
- prize eligibility
- future liquidity quality
- creator success after launch

It only marks that the campaign reached the transition point and the configured policy executed.

## Command note

Always pair finalize explanations with the current economics and readiness pages.

Do not teach one old split or one old threshold as permanent doctrine.
