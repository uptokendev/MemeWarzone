title: Graduation

---

Graduation is the step from bonding-curve fees to post-graduation trading.

It happens when the coin's bonding curve sells out, at the graduation market cap the creator picked at launch: $30K or $50K. On gen-8 BNB Chain and Robinhood Chain launches it happens in the trade that reaches that market cap. Progress shows on the coin page and as the Curve percentage on the coin card.

## What graduation changes

When a coin graduates:

1. the curve is complete
2. a 2% graduation fee comes out of what the curve raised and goes to MemeWarzone's fee routing
3. on today's launches the liquidity moves into the coin's DEX pool and is locked for good. On gen-8 BNB Chain and Robinhood Chain launches the coin stays in the same Uniswap V4 pool, so the chart and the listing on other sites continue. There is no migration.
4. the creator's 2% reserve (20M tokens) is released at graduation on today's launches. On gen-8 it can be released once the coin has been graduated for at least 1 hour and the price is at or above the graduation market cap
5. the coin card switches from LIVE to DEX
6. the Buy/Sell panel now uses post-graduation fees, and the button names the DEX

If a gen-8 coin's price later falls back below the graduation market cap, trades there pay the 2% bonding fee again.

The coin keeps its coin page, posts, holders and history.

## Where graduated coins trade

| Chain | DEX |
| --- | --- |
| BNB Chain | Topaz on today's launches. Uniswap V4 from the first second on gen-8 launches against BNB |
| Solana | Meteora |
| Robinhood Chain | a Uniswap V3 pool on today's launches. Uniswap V4 from the first second on gen-8 launches against ETH |

The token a coin pairs with in that pool was chosen at launch. On Solana and Robinhood Chain it can be something other than SOL or ETH, such as USDC or a stock token. Graduation locks the pool's liquidity for good.

## What changes for the coin

After graduation a coin can challenge other coins to battles, enter tournaments and earn Major War League points. A graduation also shows up as an auto update on the coin page and on the home feed.

After a gen-8 graduation the trade fee is 1%. The creator gets 0.5% of the trade amount. MemeWarzone gets the rest.

Coins launched before the gen-8 release keep the target, pool and fee they launched with. Solana stays as it is today. Coins paired with a stock or another quote token keep the current rules for now.
