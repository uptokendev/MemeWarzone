---

Graduation is the step from the bonding curve to a DEX pool.

It happens when the coin's bonding curve reaches its graduation target. The target is shown as progress on the coin page and as the Curve percentage on the coin card.

## What graduation changes

When a coin graduates:

1. the curve is complete and curve trading ends
2. the graduation fee from the current fee model is applied
3. the liquidity moves into the coin's DEX pool
4. the coin card switches from LIVE to DEX
5. the Buy/Sell panel now trades in the pool, and the button names the DEX

The coin keeps its coin page, posts, holders and history.

## Where graduated coins trade

| Chain | DEX |
| --- | --- |
| BNB Chain | Topaz |
| Solana | Meteora |
| Robinhood Chain | a Uniswap V3 pool |

The token a coin pairs with in that pool was chosen at launch. On Solana and Robinhood Chain it can be something other than SOL or ETH, such as USDC or a stock token. Graduation locks the pool's liquidity for good.

## What changes for the coin

After graduation a coin can challenge other coins to battles, enter tournaments and earn Major War League points. A graduation also shows up as an auto update on the coin page and on the home feed.

The target and the fee belong to the current fee model and can differ per chain and launch. Read **[Economic Model](/economics)**, **[Fee Model](/fees)** and **[War Trade Room](/traders/war-trade-room)**.
