Graduation is the step from the bonding curve to a DEX pool.

It happens when the coin's bonding curve sells out, at the graduation market cap the creator picked at launch: $30K or $50K. Progress shows on the coin page and as the Curve percentage on the coin card.

## What graduation changes

When a coin graduates:

1. the curve is complete and curve trading ends
2. a 2% graduation fee goes to MemeWarzone's fee routing
3. the liquidity moves into the coin's DEX pool and is locked for good
4. the creator's 2% reserve (20M tokens) is released
5. the coin card switches from LIVE to DEX
6. the Buy/Sell panel now trades in the pool, and the button names the DEX

The coin keeps its coin page, posts, holders and history.

## Where graduated coins trade

| Chain | DEX |
| --- | --- |
| BNB Chain | Topaz |
| Solana | Meteora |
| Robinhood Chain | a Uniswap V3 pool |

The token a coin pairs with in that pool was chosen at launch. On Solana and Robinhood Chain it can be something other than SOL or ETH, such as USDC or a stock token. Graduation locks the pool's liquidity for good.

## What changes for the coin

After graduation a coin can challenge other coins to battles, enter tournaments and earn Major War League points. A graduation also shows up as an auto update on the coin page and on the home feed.

Coins launched before the current rules keep the target and fee they launched with. Read **[Launch Rules](/creators/launch-rules)**, **[Fee Model](/fees)** and **[War Trade Room](/traders/war-trade-room)**.
