Search finds coins, drafts and people from anywhere in MemeWarzone.

![Search with an imported coin in the results](/images/docs/search.png)

## Open search

- Click the search box in the top bar. On a phone, tap the magnifier icon.
- Press **/** on your keyboard.
- Press **Ctrl+K** (Windows, Linux) or **Cmd+K** (Mac).

Type at least two characters to see results.

## What you can find

| You type | You get |
| --- | --- |
| A ticker, with or without `$` | Coins with that ticker |
| A coin name | Coins with that name |
| A token or campaign address | The coin with that address |
| A display name or `@username` | That person's profile |
| A wallet address (0x or Solana) | That wallet's profile |
| A draft name or ticker | The public draft page, marked PRE-LAUNCH |

Coin results include coins launched on MemeWarzone and imported coins. Both open the same kind of coin page.

## Which chain is searched

Coins and drafts are searched on the chain you have selected. If you picked Solana, you see Solana coins. Switch the chain with the chain buttons on Coins, Leagues or the War Trade Room, then search again.

People are found on every chain. A person has one profile per wallet, so you get one result per person, whichever chain they used.

## Before you type

With an empty search box the popup shows:

- a sponsored spot, when one is booked
- Featured coins
- Trending coins on the selected chain
- your recent searches, with a Clear button
- coins you viewed recently

Recent searches and recently viewed coins are kept in your browser only.

## Reading a result

- A coin shows its logo, name, `$TICKER` and market cap.
- A draft shows `$TICKER · PRE-LAUNCH`. It has no chart and no Buy button yet, because trading has not started.
- A person shows their picture, name, `@username` and a short wallet.

## No results

If nothing shows up:

- check the selected chain
- try the ticker on its own
- paste the full address instead of part of it
- a draft only appears once its creator made it public

Never trust a coin only because its name matches. Check the address on the coin page before you trade. Read **[Avoid Scams](/security/avoid-scams)**.
