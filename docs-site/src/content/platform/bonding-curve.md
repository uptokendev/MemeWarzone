Before graduation, native MemeWarzone campaigns trade through the launchpad bonding phase.

This is the **PRE** market. The product model is shared across all three chains while the asset used for bonding stays native to the selected network.

| Chain | PRE bonding asset |
| --- | --- |
| BNB Chain | BNB |
| Solana | SOL |
| Robinhood Chain | ETH |

## What the bonding phase does

The bonding phase gives a campaign an active market before the permanent post-graduation venue takes over.

During PRE, users can:

- buy and sell through the active launchpad route
- watch campaign progress toward the active graduation threshold
- follow market and community activity
- use discovery surfaces such as UpVotes where enabled
- generate qualifying activity for the chain-aware incentive systems

The same campaign concept applies to BNB, Solana, and Robinhood. Transaction construction, wallet prompts, and chain-level execution differ underneath.

## Graduation is separate from bonding

Bonding remains native even when the creator selected a non-native Graduation Market.

For example, Robinhood bonding remains ETH-native even if the permanent post-grad market uses another approved Robinhood quote asset. The selected Graduation Market is revalidated at the graduation boundary.

## Creator and trader protections

The active generation can enforce creator eligibility, cooldowns, launch timing, wallet restrictions, risk controls, replay protection, and chain identity.

A disabled button in the frontend is not the security boundary. Financial and irreversible rules must be enforced by the active backend/contract/program path for that chain.

## What traders should expect

PRE markets can move quickly and price impact can be significant, especially early in a campaign.

A live campaign page does not mean a token is safe or guaranteed to graduate. Verify the chain, campaign identity, amount, and wallet transaction before signing.

## Fee note

PRE trading uses the active fee model and Treasury routing for the campaign generation. Exact routing can be generation- and chain-aware; the live product and current fee documentation are authoritative.
