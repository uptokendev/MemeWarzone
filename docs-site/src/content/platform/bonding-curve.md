A coin launched on MemeWarzone starts trading on its bonding curve. The curve sets the price: buys push it up, sells push it down.

## What the bonding curve phase does

The curve gives a new coin a market from the first second. On today's launches that is before any DEX pool exists. On gen-8 BNB Chain and Robinhood Chain launches against BNB or ETH, the curve trades inside a Uniswap V4 pool from the first second. Anyone can trade it: the MemeWarzone app, other wallets, trading bots and aggregators.

While a coin is live on its curve, you can:

- buy and sell from the coin page or the War Trade Room
- watch the curve progress toward graduation on the coin page and the coin card
- follow the coin as UpVotes, posts and league standings react

## What traders should expect

Live coins move fast. Early on, one trade can move the price a lot. A busy page is not a safe page.

Read the quote before you confirm. It shows what you pay and roughly what you get.

## What creators should focus on

This is where demand shows. Keep the links official, post updates as the coin, and answer questions in the War Room chat.

## What the coin is priced in

On BNB Chain the curve trades in BNB and on Robinhood Chain in ETH. The token a coin pairs with after graduation is chosen at launch and can be different. On Solana the curve trades in the token the coin pairs with, SOL by default. Read **[Create a Campaign](/creators/create-a-campaign)**.

## Fees

Trades on the curve pay a 2% trading fee, paid in BNB or ETH on those chains, never in the coin. In the first 60 seconds after trading opens the fee starts at 90% and falls to 2%, so bots that buy at launch pay for it. The creator's first buy in the launch transaction pays 2%.

On gen-8 the creator can pick an open time up to 30 days ahead. Nobody can trade before that time, and the 90% to 2% fee starts when trading opens.
