Notifications tell you when something needs your attention, such as a challenge to your coin or activity on your posts.

## Where you see them

- **The bell** in the top bar shows your latest notifications. An orange dot marks unread ones. Tap one to open the page it is about.
- **View all** at the bottom of the bell list opens **Command Center > Notifications**, with the full list. Use **Mark all read** there to clear the unread count.

## Choosing what you get

Open **Command Center > Settings** and find the **Notification settings** card. Each category has two switches, **Bell** and **Email**:

| Category | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Battle challenges | Someone challenges your coin, declines or counters |
| Replies, reposts and @mentions | Activity on your posts and when someone tags you |
| Rewards ready to claim | League, airdrop, recruiter and battle payouts |
| Your coin events | Drafts, launch, graduation and big moments for coins you created |

Everything is on until you turn it off. Press **Save notification settings** when you are done. Saving asks your wallet to sign a message. No transaction, no fee.

Turning off **Bell** for a category hides those items from your bell.

## Email

Email switches only work once you have a verified email address. Until then they are greyed out.

1. In **Command Center > Settings**, enter your email in the **Arena challenge email** card and press **Save and verify**.
2. Open the link in the email we send you.
3. The card shows **Verified**. The Email switches are now available.

When someone challenges your coin, the challenge also shows in **Command Center > Battles**, whatever your email setting. See [Live Battles](/arena/live-battles).
