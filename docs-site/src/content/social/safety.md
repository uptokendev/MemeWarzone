Every post, reply, comment, creator update and profile has a **…** menu in its top right corner. That menu holds the safety tools.

## The "…" menu

On someone else's content the menu shows:

- **Report post**, **Report comment** or **Report profile**
- **Hide post** or **Hide comment**
- **Block** followed by the account's name, or **Unblock** if you already blocked it

Profiles have no Hide option, and creator updates have no Block option because they are posted as a coin.

On your own post or reply the menu shows only **Delete post** or **Delete reply**. See [Posting and reactions](/social/posting).

The menu works in the Home feed, on post pages, in coin page comments and creator updates, in battle comments and on profiles.

## Report

1. Choose **Report** in the "…" menu.
2. Tell us what is wrong, in 20 to 1,000 characters.
3. Press **Send report**.

Our team reviews every report. You can follow your reports in **Command Center > Support and safety > My Abuse Reports**, which lists each report with its type, date and status.

For impersonation or stolen identity, use **File Abuse Report** on the same Support and safety page.

## Hide

**Hide** removes one post or comment from your own view. It only affects you: everyone else still sees it.

## Block

**Block** hides an account for you:

- their posts, reposts and replies disappear from your feed and post pages
- their comments disappear from coin pages and battles
- their profile shows "This user is blocked" instead of their content

Blocking does not change anything else. Their coin pages and trading stay as they are, and you can still buy or sell their coins.

## Unblock

Open **Command Center > Settings** and find the **Blocked accounts** card. It lists everyone you blocked. Press **Unblock** next to a name. You can also choose **Unblock** in the "…" menu on their profile or posts.

## Signing

Report, hide and block use the same 30-day social signature as posting. If you have not signed yet, your wallet asks once. A signature costs nothing and is not a transaction. See [One signature for social actions](/social/signing).
