Home is the first page in the menu. It shows what is happening on MemeWarzone right now: people posting, coins launching, coins graduating and battles running.

![Home feed on a wide screen](/images/docs/home-feed.png)

![Home feed on a phone](/images/docs/home-feed-mobile.png)

## Layout

From top to bottom:

1. **Ad row.** Up to 6 paid banner spots. When there are more ads than fit the width, the row scrolls slowly. While a spot is free, a "Your ad here" tile links to the sponsorship application.
2. **Composer.** Write a post here. See [Posting and reactions](/social/posting).
3. **Tabs.** Pick what the feed shows.
4. **Right column** on wide screens, with people to follow, trending coins, prize cards and live battles.

## Tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| For you | Everything on MemeWarzone, newest first |
| Following | The same kinds of items, only from wallets you follow |
| Launches | Coins that just went live on the selected chain |
| Battles | Battles that are live right now |
| Graduations | Coins that graduated on the selected chain |

Phones have no right column, so its cards move into three extra tabs: **Trending**, **League** and **Recruiters**.

## For you

For you mixes every kind of item in one timeline, newest first:

- posts and reposts
- creator updates that a coin owner shared to the feed
- new launches and public drafts
- graduations
- battles that started, and battle results

Within each page, posts from wallets you follow and posts with more reactions move up a little. Keep scrolling and older items load.

### Taking off

Some posts carry a **Taking off** label. These are posts from the last 48 hours that are collecting reactions fast. Reposts count most, then replies, then rockets, then views. The top of the feed often opens with a post that is taking off.

## Following

Following shows only wallets you follow. You need a connected wallet and at least one account you follow. Use the filter chips to narrow it down:

- **Everyone:** all items from people you follow
- **People:** their plain posts
- **Coins:** creator updates and posts that mention a coin
- **Auto updates:** launches, drafts, graduations and battles

Your own posts and reposts show in For you and on your profile, not in Following.

## Right column

On wide screens the column on the right holds, in this order:

- **Who to follow:** accounts you might want to follow
- **Trending:** trending coins, with chips for All, SOL, BNB and RH
- **Weekly League:** this week's league standings
- **Weekly Airdrop:** the prize pool of the running week
- **Monthly MWL:** the Major War League prize for the month
- **Live battles:** fights happening now, with a link to all battles
- **Recruiters:** a card with a link to become a recruiter

The league, airdrop and MWL cards follow the chain you have selected. Read more in [Leagues](/leagues), [Warzone Airdrops](/rewards/warzone-airdrops) and [Major War League](/arena/major-war-league).

## Booking the ad row

The ad row is a paid placement with 6 spots. To book one, open the "Your ad here" tile and fill in the application. See [Sponsorships](/arena/sponsorships).
