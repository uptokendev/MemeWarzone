Every wallet has one public profile. It is the same profile on every chain: save it once and it shows everywhere on MemeWarzone.

![A public profile](/images/docs/public-profile.png)

Open **Profile** in the menu to see your own public profile.

## Your @username

Your @username is how people tag you in posts. Without one, nobody can tag you.

- 3 to 20 characters: letters, numbers and underscore (_).
- Unique. Case does not matter, so `Trader` and `trader` count as the same name.
- Some names are reserved, such as `admin`, `support` and `memewarzone`.
- One username per wallet, on every chain.
- You can change it once every 30 days. If you try sooner, the form tells you the date you can change it again.
- Setting or changing it asks your wallet for a signature. No transaction, no fee.

When you connect a wallet without a username, a **Pick your username** popup appears. Choose **Later** to skip it for this browser session. You can always set it in **Command Center > Edit profile**.

Your profile address also works with your username: `/profile/yourname` opens your profile.

### How names show

Across MemeWarzone a person shows as their display name. Without a display name, their @username shows. Without either, a short version of the wallet address shows.

## Edit profile

Open **Command Center > Edit profile**, or press **Edit** on your own public profile. You can set:

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Banner | PNG, JPG or WebP, max 5 MB. Wide images work best, about 1500 × 400. Use **Move image** to set which part shows |
| Picture | PNG, JPG or WebP |
| Display name | Up to 32 characters |
| @username | Set or change, with the rules above |
| Bio | Up to 280 characters. Tag collabs or your squad with @username; each tag links to that profile |
| Website | Your site |
| X | Your @handle or an x.com link |
| Telegram | Your @handle or a t.me link |

Press **Save profile**. Your wallet signs a message to prove it is you. No transaction, no fee. **Discard** drops your unsaved changes.

If you have no picture, your profile shows the default green operative.

## Your public profile

The top of a profile shows the banner, picture, name, @username, wallet address (tap to copy), an Explorer link, website, X and Telegram links, and the bio. Below that are counts for coins created, followers, following and rank.

- On someone else's profile you see **Follow** or **Unfollow**, and a **…** menu to report or block. See [Report, hide and block](/social/safety).
- On your own profile you see **Edit** and **Open Command Center**, and the composer to write a post.

### Profile tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Posts | This wallet's own posts, reposts and creator updates, newest first |
| Coins | Coins linked to this wallet, in four sections (below) |
| Drafts | Public drafts |
| Portfolio | Portfolio value and holdings figures |
| Recruiter | Recruiter details and a link to the recruiter page, when the wallet is a recruiter |
| Squad | Squad details, when the wallet is in a squad |
| Badges | Badges and rank |
| Reputation | Profile completeness and trust signals |
| Activity | Drafts, launches and trades |

Launches, graduations and battles do not show in the Posts tab. They appear in the Home feed. See [Home feed](/social/home-feed).

### Coins tab

- **Created:** coins this wallet launched on MemeWarzone
- **On MemeWarzone:** MemeWarzone coins this wallet holds
- **Imported:** imported MemeWarzone coins this wallet holds
- **In wallet:** every token in the wallet, as a list with its value

The tab opens on the first section that has something in it.

## Portfolio display settings

In **Command Center > Settings**, the **Portfolio display** card controls what your holdings lists show, in Command Center and on your public profile:

- **Hide native coins and stablecoins:** SOL, BNB, ETH, their wrapped versions, USDC, USDT and other stablecoins
- **Hide holdings under $1:** dust and tokens without a price

Both off means every holding is listed. These settings only change the lists. Your total value always counts every holding. Saving asks for a wallet signature.
