MemeWarzone has a social feed built around coins. You post, reply and react with your wallet, and every coin page has its own updates and comments.

You can read everything without a wallet. To post, react or follow, connect a wallet.

## What you can do

- **Read the Home feed.** Posts, reposts, creator updates, launches, graduations and battles in one timeline. See [Home feed](/social/home-feed).
- **Post and react.** Write posts with up to 4 images, paste a contract address to show a coin card with a Buy button, tag people with @username, reply, repost, quote and rocket. See [Posting and reactions](/social/posting).
- **Run your profile.** One profile per wallet on every chain, with a picture, banner, bio, links and a unique @username. See [Profiles and usernames](/social/profiles).
- **Post as your coin.** If you own a coin, you post updates as the coin from its page. See [Coin pages and creator updates](/social/creator-updates).
- **Choose your notifications.** Bell and email, per category. See [Notifications](/social/notifications).
- **Stay in control.** Report, hide and block from the "…" menu. See [Report, hide and block](/social/safety).

## Signing

Social actions need one wallet signature that lasts 30 days. A signature is a message, not a transaction: it costs no gas and moves no funds. See [One signature for social actions](/social/signing).

## Where things live

- **Home** is the first item in the menu and opens the feed.
- **Profile** in the menu opens your own public profile.
- **Command Center** holds your private pages: Edit profile, Notifications, Support and safety, and Settings. See [Command Center Overview](/command-center).
