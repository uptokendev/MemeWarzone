You post from the composer at the top of Home or on your own profile. Connect a wallet first. The first time you post, react or reply, your wallet asks for one signature that covers social actions for 30 days. See [One signature for social actions](/social/signing).

![A post page with its coin card and reply box](/images/docs/post-page.png)

## Writing a post

- A post can be up to **1,000 characters**. The composer counts down as you type.
- Long posts show the first 280 characters in the feed with a **Read more** link. The full text is always on the post page.
- Press **Post** to publish.

## Images

- Add up to **4 images** per post with the image button.
- PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 5 MB each.
- Remove an image before posting with the x on its preview.
- In the feed, images show as a grid. Tap one to open the viewer. Use the left and right arrows to move between images, or close the viewer to go back.

## Coin cards from a contract address

Paste a coin's contract address in your post and it turns into a coin card. The card shows:

- the coin's logo, name and chain
- the current price and its change
- a small price line
- a **Buy** button that opens the coin page

This works for coins launched on MemeWarzone and for imported coins listed on MemeWarzone. See [Imported coins](/creators/imported-coins).

## Tagging people with @username

Type **@** followed by the first letters of a name. A list of matching usernames appears; pick one to insert it. Only people who have set a username show up in the list. In the published post, every @username links to that person's profile. See [Profiles and usernames](/social/profiles).

Tagging works in the composer, in quote posts and in replies.

## Reactions

Every post has an action row:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Reply | Opens the post page, where you write a reply |
| Repost | Opens a menu with **Repost** and **Quote post** |
| Rocket | Your like. The rocket turns orange when it is on. Tap again to take it back |
| Views | How many unique viewers the post has |
| Share | Opens a menu with **Copy link** and **Share on X** |

### Repost

Repost puts the post in your followers' feeds and on your profile, with "reposted" above it. The icon turns green when it is on. Choose **Undo repost** in the same menu to take it back.

### Quote post

Quote post adds your own text on top of someone else's post. You can add up to 4 images, @mentions and a contract address, the same as a normal post. The quoted post shows as a small card inside yours.

## Replies and the post page

Open a post to see its own page. It shows:

- the post at full size, with its time and counts for reposts, rockets and replies
- a reply box ("Post your reply")
- all replies, oldest first
- a "Coin in this post" card when the post has a coin card

Replies work like posts. You can rocket, repost, quote and reply to a reply. Tap a reply's text to open its own page with the answers to it. A reply shows "Replying to a post" with a link back.

## Views

A view counts when a post is at least half on screen for about one second, or when you open its post page. Each viewer counts once per post:

- a wallet that has signed in for social actions counts once
- everyone else counts once per IP address

Opening the same post again does not add views.

## Deleting your own post or reply

Open the **…** menu on your own post or reply and choose **Delete post** or **Delete reply**. You are asked to confirm. A deleted post disappears from the feed, your profile and search. This cannot be undone.

## Following people

Open someone's profile and press **Follow**. Their posts, reposts and coin updates then show in your Following tab. Press **Unfollow** to stop. See [Home feed](/social/home-feed).
