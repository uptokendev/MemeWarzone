---
title: One signature for social actions
description: Why your wallet asks for one free signature before you post, how long it lasts, and which actions still ask every time.
---

The first time you post, reply or react, your wallet asks you to sign a message. That one signature covers your social actions for 30 days.

## A signature is not a transaction

Signing a message proves that you control the wallet. That is all it does.

- It costs nothing. There is no gas fee.
- It does not send a transaction to any chain.
- It does not move tokens or give anyone access to your funds.

The message starts with **MemeWarzone API Action**. Read the wallet prompt before you sign. If a prompt asks you to approve a transaction or spend tokens when you only wanted to post, reject it. See [Avoid Scams](/security/avoid-scams).

## What the one signature covers

For 30 days after you sign, these actions need no further signatures:

- posting, including images and coin cards
- replies
- reposts and quote posts
- rockets
- deleting your own posts and replies
- comments on posts, coin pages and battles
- War Room chat
- reports
- block and hide
- creator updates on a coin you own

The signature is kept in this browser, so it survives closing the tab. On another browser or device, or with another wallet, you sign once more. After 30 days your wallet asks again the next time you do one of these actions.

## What still asks every time

Actions that change your account, your coin or your funds keep their own signature or transaction each time:

- setting or changing your @username
- saving Edit profile
- notification settings
- portfolio display settings
- coin page profile, Story text and banner
- ownership claims for imported coins
- payments
- reward claims
- votes
- battle challenges

Saving a setting is a signed message, with no fee. Payments, claims and other actions that move funds are transactions, and your wallet shows the cost before you approve.

## Following needs nothing

**Follow** and **Unfollow** on a profile need a connected wallet but no signature.
