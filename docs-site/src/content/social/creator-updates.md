Every coin page has a **Posts** tab. It holds updates from the coin's owner, automatic updates, comments and the War Room chat for that coin.

![A coin page](/images/docs/coin-page.png)

## Who counts as the owner

- For a coin launched on MemeWarzone: the wallet that created it.
- For an imported coin: the verified owner of the import. See [Imported coins](/creators/imported-coins).

Only the owner's connected wallet sees the update composer and the edit options.

## Posting a creator update

At the top of the Posts tab the owner sees **Post an update to your holders**.

- You post **as the coin**: the update shows the coin's logo and name, with a **Creator update** label.
- Up to 280 characters.
- Up to 4 images (PNG, JPG or WebP).
- **Also in the home feed:** tick it to share the update to the Home feed as well. Whether it starts ticked depends on your page setting (below).
- Press **Post**.

Creator updates use the same 30-day social signature as posts. See [One signature for social actions](/social/signing).

To remove an update, the owner uses the delete button on it. A deleted update can no longer be pinned.

### Reactions on creator updates

Creator updates have the same action row as feed posts: replies, reposts, quote posts, rockets, views and share. See [Posting and reactions](/social/posting).

## Auto updates

The coin page also posts updates on its own, marked **Auto update**:

- the launch
- the graduation
- battles, with a **View battle** link

The owner can turn these off on the edit page.

## Editing the coin page

The owner opens the edit page from **Command Center > My coins > Edit page**. Name, ticker and image are part of the token itself and cannot change. Everything else on the edit page can:

| Section | What you set |
|---|---|
| Banner | Upload a banner (1500 × 500, JPG, PNG or WebP, max 5 MB). Drag it or use the slider to set its position |
| About | Bio (up to 1,200 characters, launched coins only), founder note (up to 140 characters), Website, X, Telegram, Discord, and up to 5 tags |
| Story | Short story, the full story boxes, and one image per box |
| Posts | Pinned post, "Share my updates to the home feed by default", "Show auto updates (battles, launch, graduation)" |

What these do:

- **Bio:** its paragraphs also become your Story slides. Empty keeps your launch description.
- **Founder note:** the quote on the cover of your Story.
- **Links:** replace the launch links on the coin page. An empty field keeps the launch link.
- **Tags:** shown next to the contract address on the coin page.
- **Pinned post:** one of your updates, shown at the top of the Posts tab with a **Pinned** label.

Imported coins edit their description with the **EDIT** button on the coin page instead of the bio field.

Saving asks your wallet to sign a message. No transaction, no fee. Page fields and Story text are signed separately, and only when they changed.

## Comments

Under the updates, the Posts tab has **Comments** and **Creator Updates** sections. Anyone with a connected wallet can comment, up to 500 characters. Comments use the 30-day social signature. Use the **…** menu on a comment to report it, hide it or block its author. See [Report, hide and block](/social/safety).

## War Room chat

Each coin has a live War Room chat. Reading needs no wallet. To write, connect a wallet and sign once to join. After that, messages need no extra signatures. Messages from the coin's creator carry a **Creator** label. See [War Room Chat](/platform/war-room).
