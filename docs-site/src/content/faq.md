## What is MemeWarzone?

MemeWarzone is a competitive on-chain ecosystem built around the full memecoin lifecycle: Prepare, Launch or Import, Trade, Graduate, Compete, Recruit, Earn, Battle, Tournament, League, and Return.

The product is designed so graduation does not have to be the end of a project's visibility or community activity.

## Which chains does MemeWarzone support?

MemeWarzone has three first-class product chains:

- **BNB Chain**
- **Solana**
- **Robinhood Chain**

Robinhood is integrated into the same product model as BNB and Solana. That includes native campaign creation, Draft / Prepare Mode, Direct and scheduled launch behavior, ETH bonding, Graduation Market selection, V3 post-grad markets, existing-token Import, rewards/claims identity, and Warzone chain identity.

Individual production financial rails can still be enabled or paused independently for operational safety.

Read **[Chain Readiness](/platform/chain-readiness)** and **[Robinhood Operations](/platform/robinhood-operations)**.

## Can I join MemeWarzone if my token already launched somewhere else?

Yes.

Use **[Import an Existing Token](/import)** to register an existing **BNB, Solana, or Robinhood** token without relaunching it or creating a second ticker.

## What does Import do?

Import creates or claims a MemeWarzone project page around an existing token.

It can include project identity, image, description, website, X, Telegram, ownership status, sharing, and supported community surfaces.

Import does not fabricate a MemeWarzone launch campaign, bonding history, graduation, or creator economics.

## Does Robinhood Import work differently?

Robinhood uses the EVM ownership model at the product layer, similar to BNB, while keeping chain 4663 identity and Robinhood token data separate.

Where current ownership is exposed by the contract, the connected signed wallet must match that authority. Manual project ownership review remains available when automatic proof cannot be established.

Robinhood Import is independent from Robinhood native launch, graduation, V3 trading, claims, and Arena activation.

## What does OWNER VERIFIED mean?

OWNER VERIFIED means the current project-page ownership process has verified who is allowed to control the MemeWarzone project page.

It does not mean the token was launched by MemeWarzone, is risk-free, is audited, is approved for a Graduation Market, or is admitted to Arena competition.

## What if automatic ownership verification is unavailable?

A project can request manual ownership review.

Automatic verification being unavailable does not automatically reject the project. Manual project ownership review remains separate from Arena approval.

## Why does an imported project say WARZONE ACCESS LOCKED?

Because project onboarding and financial Arena access are separate.

A project can be imported and owner-verified while Battles, Tournaments, Major War League, Quarterly Championship, Boost payments, Arena claims, and imported-token trading remain disabled or not yet approved for that project.

## What is a native MemeWarzone campaign?

A native campaign is the product object created through the MemeWarzone launch system. It carries the token's launch generation, chain, PRE market, graduation state, post-grad venue, community identity, and later competition history.

An imported project is different: it represents an existing token without pretending that token was launched through MemeWarzone.

## Can I launch natively on Robinhood?

Yes. Robinhood is part of the same full launchpad architecture as BNB and Solana.

The Robinhood lifecycle is:

**Prepare → Direct / Scheduled Launch → ETH Bonding → Graduation Market → V3 Post-Grad Market → Rewards / Claims → Warzone**

Read **[Robinhood Operations](/platform/robinhood-operations)**.

## What is Direct?

Direct is the fast native-launch route for a project that is ready to deploy on the selected chain.

## What is Draft or Prepare Mode?

Draft / Prepare Mode is the pre-deployment lane where a creator can build the project identity, content, community, timing, and launch settings before deployment.

## What is PRE?

PRE is the native campaign's bonding / pre-graduation market state.

Bonding is chain-native: BNB on BNB Chain, SOL on Solana, and ETH on Robinhood Chain.

## What is POST?

POST is the native campaign's post-graduation market stage.

The current venue architecture is Topaz on BNB, Meteora on Solana, and chain-local V3 infrastructure on Robinhood.

## What is Graduation Market?

Graduation Market is the creator-selected permanent quote market used after native bonding reaches the graduation threshold.

The selected quote is chain-specific, bound by exact asset identity, and revalidated at graduation. MemeWarzone does not silently replace an unsafe selected non-native quote with the chain's native wrapped asset.

## What is Arena?

Arena is MemeWarzone's post-graduation competitive layer for Normal Battles, Tournaments, Major War League, Quarterly Championship, Boosts, sponsorships, and related claims.

Arena identity remains chain-specific for BNB, Solana, and Robinhood.

## How does V3 Battle scoring work?

Current V3 Normal Battles use 45 points of market-cap growth, 27 holders, 18 eligible real volume, and up to 10 Community Boost points.

Read **[Battle Scoring & Boosts](/arena/scoring-and-boosts)** for the exact Boost curve.

## What is a Vote Tournament?

Vote Tournament uses a 24-hour voting regulation format instead of Normal Battle market metrics.

A free vote is 1 Vote Point. A $1 paid Boost is 2 Vote Points. Exact regulation ties move into Final Salvo.

## What is Quarterly Championship?

Quarterly Championship is a continuous chain-specific standing across the quarter.

It is not a quarterfinal / semifinal / final knockout tournament.

## Does an UpVote mean a campaign is safe?

No.

An UpVote is a visibility/discovery action, not a safety guarantee.

## Are rewards automatic?

No.

User-visible financial rewards move through their authoritative settlement and claim paths. A claim must be chain-correct, replay-safe, and exactly-once.

BNB rewards settle in BNB, Solana rewards in SOL, and Robinhood rewards in ETH where the applicable reward rail is active.

## Where should I claim?

Only through official MemeWarzone product pages.

Never trust random claim prompts from copied links or direct messages.

## Do fee and reward values stay fixed forever?

No.

Economics can be generation- or subsystem-specific. Always use the current Fee Model, Arena rules, Treasury, and Rewards pages for the rail you are using.
