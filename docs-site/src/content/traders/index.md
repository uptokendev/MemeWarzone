Traders turn attention into market action. You find a coin, check it, trade it, and follow it as it moves from the bonding curve to a DEX pool and into battles.

![The Coins page](/images/docs/coins-page.png)

## The trader flow

1. find a coin on Coins, Home, search or the War Trade Room
2. check the chain and the coin's state: live, graduated or imported
3. read the coin page: links, chart, holders, posts
4. trade only when the setup is clear
5. follow the coin, its posts and its battles

## What to watch

- the selected chain and your connected wallet
- the coin's state and its curve progress
- the official links
- the holders and recent trades
- posts from the coin's owner
- battles the coin is in
- rewards waiting in your Command Center

## Where traders spend time

- **Coin page**: chart, metrics, Buy/Sell panel, Posts, Trades, Holders
- **War Trade Room**: a table of coins where each row opens a chart and a trade panel
- **Home**: posts, launches, graduations and live battles
- **Warzone**: vote and boost in battles
- **Command Center**: your portfolio, claims and airdrops
