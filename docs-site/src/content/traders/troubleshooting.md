title: Troubleshooting
description: Common trading problems and how to fix them.
---

## Transaction is pending

- Give it a few minutes. The network can be busy.
- Your wallet may offer **Speed up** or **Cancel**.

## Buy or sell fails

Common reasons:

- the price moved more than your slippage allows
- not enough BNB, SOL or ETH left for the network fee
- the order is large and moves the price too much
- your wallet is on a different network than the coin

What to do:

- try a smaller amount
- keep some of the chain's coin for network fees
- check that your wallet is on the coin's chain
- try again with slightly higher slippage, if the panel offers it

## Balance does not update

- Refresh the page.
- Check that the right wallet is connected.
- Check in your wallet history that the transaction succeeded.

## I cannot find a coin

- Check that you are on the official site.
- Check the selected chain. Coins and search show one chain at a time.
- Search by ticker, name or the full address. Read **[Search](/platform/search)**.

## Suspected scam or fake link

- Do not connect your wallet.
- Do not sign messages you do not understand.
- Use only official channels and bookmarked links.
