Edit profile is where you change what people see on your public profile. On your own public profile, the grey **Edit** button opens this tab.

![The Edit profile tab](/images/docs/command-center-edit-profile.png)

One wallet has one profile. It shows the same on every chain.

## What you can change

| Field | Rules |
|---|---|
| Banner | PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 5 MB. Wide images work best, about 1500 × 400. Use the **Move image** slider to choose which part shows. |
| Picture | PNG, JPG or WebP. Without one, your profile shows the green operative. |
| Display name | Up to 32 characters. |
| Username | Your @username. See below. |
| Bio | Up to 280 characters. An @username in your bio links to that profile. |
| Website, X, Telegram | A link or a handle, for example @handle or an x.com link. |

## Username

Your @username is how people tag you in posts. Without one, nobody can tag you.

- 3 to 20 characters: letters, numbers and underscores.
- It must be unique. Some names are reserved.
- You can change it once every 30 days. After a change, the tab shows the date of your next possible change.

Press **Set username** or **Change**, pick a name, and the form tells you if it is available. Saving the username asks for its own signature.

## Saving

Uploading a picture or banner does not save it yet. Press **Save profile** to apply all changes. Your wallet asks you to sign a message. It is not a transaction and costs no fee. **Discard** throws away unsaved changes.

**View public profile** opens your profile so you can check the result.
