Overview is the first tab in Command Center. It shows what the connected wallet holds on the selected chain.

![The Overview tab](/images/docs/command-center-overview.png)

## Balance tiles

- **Native**: the balance of the chain's own coin in your wallet, such as BNB or SOL.
- **Coins held**: how many tokens the wallet holds, not counting the native coin.

## Portfolio

Four tiles sit at the top of the Portfolio card:

| Tile | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Total value | The USD value of everything in the wallet, native coin included. |
| Top holding | Your biggest holding by value, and its share of the portfolio. |
| MemeWarzone coins | How many coins you hold that were launched or imported on MemeWarzone. The line below counts your other tokens. |
| Wallet age | How long the wallet has been active, with the start date. |

## Top holdings

Below the tiles is a list of up to eight holdings. Each row shows the logo, ticker, name, balance and USD value. The native coin is listed too.

A row is labelled **Launched here**, **Imported**, **Native coin** or **Stablecoin** where that applies. Coins launched or imported on MemeWarzone open their coin page when you tap them. Other tokens are listed only.

## Hide small or stable holdings

Go to **Settings > Portfolio display** to change what the list shows:

- **Hide native coins and stablecoins** hides SOL, BNB, ETH, their wrapped versions, USDC, USDT and other stablecoins.
- **Hide holdings under $1** hides dust and tokens without a price.

These settings also apply to the holdings on your public profile. Total value always counts every holding, whatever you hide.

## Daily briefing

If you own a coin in the Major War League, a Daily briefing card appears at the top. It has two buttons: **Daily check-in** and **War Dispatch**, which opens a post to X. Both add league points once your coin has finished a battle this quarter. See [Major War League](/arena/major-war-league).

## League cabinet

The League cabinet card counts the trophies and badges your wallet has earned in leagues. Until you win something it says "No trophies yet".

Read [Settings](/command-center/settings) for the display settings and [Rewards and claims](/command-center/claims) to collect rewards.
