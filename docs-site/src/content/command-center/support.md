**Support and safety** is the help tab in Command Center. It has three parts: help questions, Discord support and abuse reports.

## Search the help questions

Type in **Search MemeWarzone Help** to search every help question. Without a search, pick a category: Popular, Wallets, Create & Launch, Trading, Rewards, Community, Recruiters & Squads or Safety. Tap a question to read the answer.

## Still need help? Use Discord

Questions about wallets, trading, launches, rewards and the product go to the MemeWarzone Discord. The **Still need help?** card has the link.

## Report abuse

Abuse reports are for problems with identity, not with the product:

- Someone is impersonating you.
- Someone is using your images or content.
- A fake project or token is pretending to represent you.
- Phishing or scams that pretend to be you or MemeWarzone.

A failed trade or a missing reward is not abuse. Take those to Discord.

### File a report

Press **File Abuse Report** to open the form:

1. Pick a category.
2. Pick what you are reporting if you know it: a profile, campaign, token, wallet, feed post, external account, external website or other. Add the link or address.
3. Describe what happened, where, and how it targets you.
4. Enter your email. It is only used to tell you when your report is updated.
5. Add evidence if you have it: JPG, PNG, WEBP or PDF, up to 5 files of 10 MB each.

After you submit, you get a case number and a link to the case.

You can also report a post, comment or profile straight from its **…** menu. See [Report, hide and block](/social/safety).

### Your reports and replies

**My Abuse Reports** lists the cases filed from this wallet, with type, date, status and last update. Open a case to see the facts, the conversation with the abuse desk, and a reply box where you can add text and more evidence.

Staff replies appear only here. You get an email when there is a reply, but do not answer the email. Reply inside the case. Only you and the abuse team can see your reports. Regular Discord support cannot.

When a case is closed, the reply box is replaced by "Case closed".

Read [Avoid Scams](/security/avoid-scams).
