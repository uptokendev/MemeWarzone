Settings holds the controls for your account. Your picture, name, @username, banner and bio are not here. They are on the [Edit profile](/command-center/edit-profile) tab.

![The Settings tab](/images/docs/command-center-settings.png)

## Wallets

Shows the owner wallet address and the chain your wallet is on. If the wallet is on a network MemeWarzone does not support, a **Switch network** button appears.

Two shortcuts sit below: **Public profile** and **Create coin**.

## Arena challenge email

Add an email address if you want a copy when someone challenges your coin. Press **Save and verify**, sign the message in your wallet, then open the link in the email you receive. The chip next to the button shows **Awaiting verification** or **Verified**.

Challenges always show in the Battles tab too, with or without an email.

## Portfolio display

Two switches decide what your holdings lists show, in Command Center and on your public profile:

- **Hide native coins and stablecoins**: SOL, BNB, ETH, their wrapped versions, USDC, USDT and other stablecoins.
- **Hide holdings under $1**: dust and tokens without a price.

Total value always counts every holding. Saving asks for a wallet signature.

## Blocked accounts

Lists the accounts you have blocked, with an **Unblock** button for each. Blocking hides that account's posts and comments for you. Their coin pages and trading are not affected. See [Report, hide and block](/social/safety).

## Notification settings

Each category has two switches: **Bell** for the notification bell and the Notifications tab, and **Email**.

| Category | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Battle challenges | Someone challenges your coin, declines or counters. |
| Replies, reposts and @mentions | Activity on your posts and when someone tags you. |
| Rewards ready to claim | League, airdrop, recruiter and battle payouts. |
| Your coin events | Drafts, launch, graduation and big moments for coins you created. |

Email switches only work once you have a verified email address. Change the switches, then press **Save notification settings** and sign in your wallet. See [Notifications](/social/notifications).

## Signatures

Saving your email, portfolio display or notification settings asks your wallet to sign a message. It is not a transaction and costs no fee. Unblocking uses your social signature, which covers 30 days. See [One signature for social actions](/social/signing).
