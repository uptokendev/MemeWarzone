title: Notifications tab
description: The Notifications tab lists the notifications for your wallet. Which ones you get is set in Settings.
---

The Notifications tab is the full list of notifications for your wallet. The bell in the top bar shows the latest ones. **View all** in the bell opens this tab.

## The list

Each notification shows a title, a short text and the date. Unread ones have an orange dot. Tap a notification to open the page it is about. That also marks it as read.

**Mark all read** at the top marks every notification as read. It shows how many are unread.

If the list is empty, it says "No notifications yet".

## Choose what you get

The switches are not on this tab. They are in **Settings > Notification settings**, with a Bell and an Email switch for each category:

- Battle challenges
- Replies, reposts, quotes, rockets, follows and @mentions
- Rewards ready to claim
- Your coin events

A category with its Bell switch off is not listed in the bell or on this tab. Email needs a verified email address. See [Settings](/command-center/settings).

Read [Notifications](/social/notifications) for what triggers each notification.
